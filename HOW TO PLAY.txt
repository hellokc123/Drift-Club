DRIFT CLUB - HOW TO PLAY ON A WINDOWS COMPUTER (no admin needed)
=================================================================

SOLO PLAY (nothing to install)
  1. Right-click the downloaded drift-club.zip and choose "Extract All...", then click Extract.
     (Opening the zip without extracting can stop the files from working.)
  2. Open the extracted "drift-game" folder.
  3. Double-click  DriftClub.html
     - If it opens in the wrong program: right-click it > Open with > Microsoft Edge (or Google Chrome).
  4. Click anywhere once to enable sound, then choose PLAY.

  Everything works this way: all 5 maps, all 4 free modes, the 3 competitive modes (vs CPU / ghost), the garage, settings and your
  saved car/progress. Only multiplayer needs the server below.

MULTIPLAYER ONLINE (friends on other computers)
  Multiplayer needs the small room server running somewhere everyone can reach.
  Do this ONCE (about 10 minutes, all in the browser, free, no admin):
  1. github.com: sign up, click "+" > New repository, name it drift-club, Public, Create.
     Click "uploading an existing file", drag in EVERYTHING inside the drift-game folder
     (server.js, package.json, render.yaml, the public folder, ...), click Commit changes.
  2. render.com: sign up with GitHub. Click New + > Blueprint, pick drift-club, click Apply.
     After 1-2 minutes you get an address like  https://drift-club-ab12.onrender.com
  3. Open that address in Edge or Chrome - it's the full game, already online.
     MULTIPLAYER > Create a room, then send your friends the address + the 5-letter room code.
     (Using DriftClub.html instead? Go to MULTIPLAYER > Server and paste the address once.)
  Notes: the free server sleeps when nobody plays; the first visit can take up to a minute
  ("Waking up..."). If your connection drops, the game reconnects you automatically.
  If the school network blocks onrender.com, play from home.

SAME NETWORK ONLY (optional): Start-Multiplayer-Server.bat with portable Node.js
  (Windows Binary .zip from nodejs.org extracted into this folder - no admin). Friends on
  the same network open http://YOUR-IP:3000. School networks often block this.

DRIVING STYLES & TRICKS
  Garage > Driving style: DRIFT (easy slides, adjustable max angle) or RACE (grip, precise,
  extra steering lock for tight corners; the steering range narrows smoothly at high speed).
  Switch any time from the pause menu (Esc).
  Drift: tap Space while steering into a corner. Throttle = more angle. Lift off to straighten.
  Transition: hold the opposite direction while drifting (Race: + tap Space).
  360: while drifting, hold Space + steer into the slide; release after a full turn.
  Reverse drift: keep turning past 180 and let go; steer to hold it, S keeps the speed.
  Donuts: slow down, Space + full lock + full throttle, keep holding.

GARAGE  (customize + tune)
  Looks: paint, wheels, rim size, bumpers, skirts, body kits, bonnet, spoiler, exhaust,
         tint, headlights, livery, stripes, underglow.  Drag the car to rotate it.
  Performance: engine, gearbox, tyres, suspension, brakes, weight.
  Tuning: sliders for gearing, ride height, suspension, steering, brakes and drift/grip.
  Presets: Stock, Street, Sport, Racing, Drift, Grip, Speed, or save your own (per car).
  The bars compare Stock with your setup.  They are measured by test-driving the car.
  Online rooms can be Open mods, Equalized or Stock only (shown before you join).

MINIMAP  (Settings > Minimap: size, corner, rotating or north-up, zoom.  N = on/off)

COMPETITIVE MODES  (PLAY > top row,  RECORDS on the main menu)
  Drift Challenge  Timed (best total, 2 min) or Score Attack (best single chain).
                   Crashes -250, leaving the track -150. Spinning on the spot scores nothing.
  Speed Race       Laps around a circuit. Drive through every gate in order (the glowing
                   pillars). Solo: race CPU cars. Online: race your friends.
  Drift Race       A race on the Speed Race circuits where drifting pays. Score = drift
                   points (until you finish, combo banks every lap) + position bonus
                   (P1 +40,000 per lap, P2 +30,000, P3 +22,000 ...). Don't finish = no bonus.
  Drift Battle     1 v 1. Run 1 you lead, run 2 you chase, then roles swap. Both runs count.
                   Solo: you chase a ghost of your own lead run.
  Online: pick the mode's card > Play online (or choose it when creating a room).
  The server keeps time and scores, so everyone sees the same results. People who join
  mid-match watch (C switches player) and play the next one. Rematch on the results screen.
  Your personal bests are saved in the browser. Online leaderboards reset when the free
  Render server is redeployed.

CONTROLS
  W / S        accelerate / brake & reverse      Space   handbrake (start drifts)
  A / D        steer                              Shift   boost
  C camera     R reset car     Esc pause menu     H hide HUD     M music on/off     N minimap
  Xbox / PlayStation controllers work too (RT accelerate, LT brake, left stick steer, A handbrake).
